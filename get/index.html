Pick your device below to install the beta.

You're browsing inside another app, which sometimes blocks App Store / Play Store links. If a button below doesn't work, tap the ··· or share icon and choose Open in Safari (or Chrome), then reopen this page.
1

Install TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's own app for trying out betas — it's how you'll get the Korvi app before it's on the public App Store.

Get TestFlight
2

Open your invite email

Check your email on your iPhone or iPad for a message from Korvi (via TestFlight). Tap the link inside it — it'll open in TestFlight automatically.

Don't see it? Check spam, or ask us to resend it.

3

Tap Install

TestFlight will show a green Accept button, then Install. Once it's done, Korvi appears on your home screen just like any other app.

A couple of things to know: you'll need to already be signed in with an Apple ID on your device (no extra account needed). Beta versions like this one expire after 90 days — if that happens, just ask us and we'll send a fresh invite.
1

Join the beta

Tap below and then tap Become a tester. You'll need to be signed in with the Google account we've added — if this doesn't work, let us know and we'll add you.

Join the beta
2

Install from the Play Store

Once you've joined, install Korvi like any other app.

Install Korvi
Good to know: it can take a few minutes after joining the beta before the Play Store lets you install — if the button says the app isn't available yet, wait a moment and try again.
