Our mission

Korvi exists to get households back to home cooking.

We want to take the pain out of meal planning and supermarket shopping: share the load across the household, and cut down the decisions nobody wants to make at six o'clock on a Tuesday.

Cook something different every night.

Households

Share the load

In most homes, one person quietly carries the planning, the listing and the remembering. Korvi puts that work where everyone can see it, on one shared plan and one shared list. Helping isn't "tell me what to buy" any more; it's just opening the app.

Auto-fill

Can't face deciding?
Roll the dice.

One tap fills your empty days with recipes drawn from a pool you control: favourites, your try-later list, your own recipes, or the whole catalogue, filtered against your allergen profile. It's a starting point rather than a lock-in, so swap anything you don't fancy.

Recipes

Fork a recipe,
make it yours

Every recipe in Korvi can be forked, like software. Prefer your carbonara with pecorino, or a different cheese entirely? Fork it, tweak it, save your version, and the original stays put. Add your own by hand, from pasted text, or from a link.

Shopping

One list for
the whole shop

Korvi builds the list from your week's meals, then lets you add the rest: weekly staples, household essentials, one-off extras. It covers the whole shop, not just the recipes. Tick something off and it's gone from their list too, before they reach the aisle.

How it works

From "what's for dinner?"
to done in three steps

Plan once, cook all week.

Plan the week

Pick a meal for each day, or mark the days you're not cooking. Can't decide? Tap the dice and Korvi fills the empty days. Everyone in the household sees the plan the moment it changes.

Shop the list

Korvi turns the week into a shopping list, grouped in the order you walk your shop. Add your weekly staples and one-offs, then tick things off together, even from two trolleys at once.

Cook your meal

Cook mode walks you through one step at a time, with the ingredient quantities for that step and built-in timers. It works offline, flour-covered fingers and all.
