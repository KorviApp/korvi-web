Korvi support

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Contact us

Email hello@korvi.eu for help with anything in the app. For privacy questions, or to use your data rights, email privacy@korvi.eu.

When you write, tell us which phone you use (iPhone or Android) and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps. Don't send your password.

Korvi Plus

What does it cost? Korvi Plus costs €2.99 a month, including VAT. New subscribers get the first 7 days free; the App Store or Google Play decides whether you're eligible. If you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing.

Does it renew? Yes, every month at the current price, until you cancel.

How do I cancel? In the App Store or Google Play, not in Korvi. Open your subscriptions there and cancel Korvi Plus at least 24 hours before the next renewal. It then keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting your Korvi account does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund? Ask Apple or Google through your store account. They handle payments, so we can't refund you ourselves. If you're an EU consumer and need help with your right to withdraw, email hello@korvi.eu. The details are in section 6 of the Terms.

Who is it for? The whole household. One subscription covers everyone in it while it's paid for.

Deleting your account

You can do it in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account. It happens straight away. Read what is deleted and what stays, or how to delete only some of your data.

Reporting a recipe

If a public recipe is wrong, unsafe or offensive, open the recipe and tap Report this recipe in the last row of the page. Pick a reason and, if you like, add a note. Your household won't see that recipe again. A person on our team looks at every report within 48 hours. The recipe's author isn't told who reported it.

If you're the author of a recipe we removed and you think we got it wrong, reply to the email we sent you, or write to hello@korvi.eu.

Privacy

Our Privacy Policy says what Korvi collects, why, where it's stored, how long we keep it and what your rights are. Usage data is only sent if you agree, and you can change your mind any time in Settings, under Usage data. Questions: privacy@korvi.eu.
